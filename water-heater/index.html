Good to know

Water heater questions, answered

How much does a new water heater cost?+
It depends on the type (tank vs. tankless), size, and fuel. We give you a firm, flat-rate price in person before any work begins — and 0% APR financing is available so you can spread the cost.
Tank or tankless — which is right for me?+
Tankless gives you endless hot water and lower energy bills but costs more upfront; a tank is a lower-cost, fast swap. On the free estimate we'll size both to your home and let you decide — no pressure.
Can you replace it today?+
Most standard tank replacements and many tankless installs are same-day or next-day across metro Atlanta. Call us and we'll tell you exactly when we can be there.
Do you offer financing?+
Yes — 0% APR financing with quick approval on qualifying replacements. Ask when you call and we'll get you prequalified in minutes.
What areas do you serve?+
All of metro Atlanta — including Sandy Springs, Marietta, Roswell, Alpharetta, Decatur, Dunwoody, Johns Creek, Kennesaw, Smyrna, and more. Not sure? Just call.
